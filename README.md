# Auto Split 🛺
### Shared expenses, made simple.

**Auto Split is a production-deployed web application that helps people track travel fares, split shared expenses, manage budgets, and understand their spending — all in one place.**

🌐 **Live Application:** https://autofaretracker.netlify.app/

---

## Overview

Keeping track of shared expenses shouldn't be complicated.

When friends share an auto ride, split a food bill, or contribute towards everyday costs, remembering who paid and calculating everyone's share can quickly become inconvenient.

**Auto Split brings these tasks together in one organised experience.** It combines travel and food expense tracking with group cost-sharing, weekly budgets, settlements, and spending analytics.

The application is publicly accessible through its live website, allowing anyone to visit and use the features available to them.

## Features

### 🛺 Travel Expense Tracking
Record everyday journeys and keep travel costs organised.

- Record fares and trip details.
- Categorise different modes of transport.
- Track passengers and payment details.
- Calculate individual shares for shared journeys.
- Edit and review existing trip records.
- Use presets for recurring journeys.

### 🍔 Food Expense Management
Keep food spending organised alongside travel expenses.

- Record food purchases and expenses.
- Categorise spending.
- Track participants and who paid.
- Calculate shares for group bills.
- Reuse presets for frequently repeated purchases.

### 🤝 Shared Expenses and Settlements
Make group spending easier to manage.

- Calculate each participant's share.
- Track contributions and payment balances.
- Review outstanding settlements.
- Organise shared travel and food expenses.
- Reduce manual calculations when splitting bills.

### 💰 Weekly Budgets
Stay aware of your spending throughout the week.

- Set separate travel and food budgets.
- Monitor expenses against your limits.
- Track your progress throughout the week.
- Use available budget alerts to stay informed.

### 📊 Spending Analytics
Understand where your money goes through clear expense summaries.

- Review total expenses and individual shares.
- Track out-of-pocket spending.
- Compare spending across days.
- View per-person breakdowns.
- Explore available weekly and monthly summaries.

### 📅 History and Search
Find previous transactions and review past spending.

- Browse trip and food records.
- Navigate between available weeks and months.
- Search through supported expense details.
- Filter records by available categories.
- Review historical logs.

### 📤 Reports and Exports
Keep useful records of your expenses.

- Export supported records to CSV.
- Generate PDF reports where available.
- Copy expense logs for convenient reference.

### 🔔 Reminders and Notifications
Use supported reminders to maintain your expense-tracking routine.

Available options may include daily logging reminders, weekly summaries, settlement reminders, and budget alerts. Browser permissions and configuration may be required.

### 🎨 Personalisation
Adapt the application to your preferences using available themes, profile options, settings, and reusable presets.

### 🔐 Sign-In
Google sign-in is available for features that require authentication. Access requirements depend on the feature and application configuration.

## Who Can Use Auto Split?

Auto Split is designed for anyone who wants to organise personal or shared expenses.

- **Friends and groups** sharing travel fares and food bills.
- **Students** managing everyday spending with friends.
- **Daily commuters** tracking recurring transport costs.
- **Individuals** maintaining personal expense records.
- **Small groups** keeping track of contributions and settlements.

## Getting Started

1. Visit the [Auto Split website](https://autofaretracker.netlify.app/).
2. Sign in if prompted.
3. Set up the available profile or group options.
4. Start recording travel or food expenses.
5. Configure budgets and review spending summaries.
6. Use the available settlement and reporting features to manage your records.

No local installation is required to use the hosted application.

## Why Auto Split?

Shared expenses are part of everyday life, but keeping them organised can become frustrating. Forgotten transactions, unclear payment responsibilities, and repeated calculations make it difficult to maintain an accurate picture of spending.

Auto Split aims to simplify that process by bringing expense recording, cost sharing, budgeting, and analytics into a single application.

**The goal is simple: make shared spending easier to track, understand, and manage.**
