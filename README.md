## Overview

**Auto Split** is a publicly accessible, production-deployed expense management application designed to make personal and shared spending easier to organise.

From splitting an auto fare with friends to tracking food expenses and monitoring weekly budgets, Auto Split brings everyday expense management into one interface.

It combines transaction tracking, group expense splitting, settlement management, spending analytics, and exportable reports to help users maintain a clearer picture of their expenses.

Whether you're managing your own spending or sharing costs with a group, Auto Split provides a central place to record expenses and review financial activity.

## Features

### 🛺 Travel Expense Tracking

Keep everyday journeys organised and accessible.

- Record travel fares and trip details.
- Categorise supported transport types.
- Track passengers and payment information.
- Calculate individual shares for shared journeys.
- Edit and review recorded trips.
- Use reusable presets for recurring journeys.

### 🍔 Food Expense Management

Manage food purchases and shared meal expenses alongside travel costs.

- Record food expenses and descriptions.
- Organise purchases using available categories.
- Track participants and payment details.
- Calculate individual shares for shared bills.
- Reuse presets for frequently repeated expenses.

### 🤝 Expense Splitting and Settlements

Make group spending easier to understand.

- Calculate each participant's share of an expense.
- Track who paid and how costs are distributed.
- Review outstanding balances and settlements.
- Organise shared travel and food expenses.
- Reduce manual calculations when splitting bills.

### 💰 Weekly Budgets

Monitor everyday spending against personal limits.

- Configure weekly travel and food budgets.
- Track spending against budget limits.
- Review progress throughout the week.
- Use supported budget alerts to stay informed.

### 📊 Spending Analytics

Turn expense records into useful spending insights.

- Review total expenses and individual shares.
- Track out-of-pocket spending.
- Compare spending across different days.
- Explore per-person expense breakdowns.
- Review available weekly and monthly summaries.

### 📅 History, Search, and Filters

Find previous transactions and understand past spending.

- Browse recorded trips and expenses.
- Navigate between available weeks and months.
- Search records using supported details.
- Filter transactions by available categories.
- Review historical expense logs.

### 📤 Reports and Data Export

Keep copies of your expense records for reference.

- Export supported records as CSV.
- Generate PDF reports where available.
- Copy expense logs for convenient review and sharing.

### 🔔 Reminders and Notifications

Use available reminders to maintain your expense-tracking routine.

Supported options may include daily logging reminders, weekly summaries, settlement reminders, and budget alerts. Browser permissions and application settings may be required.

### 🎨 Personalisation

Customise the experience using available preferences.

- Choose supported visual themes.
- Configure available profile and avatar options.
- Create reusable expense presets.
- Adjust supported settings to suit your workflow.

### 🔐 Sign-In and Access

Auto Split is publicly accessible through its live website. Google sign-in is available for features that require authentication.

Access requirements depend on the feature and application configuration.

## Design Philosophy

Auto Split focuses on making everyday expense management more organised and understandable.

Its approach centres on:

- **Simplicity** — making expense recording straightforward.
- **Clarity** — showing who paid and how expenses are divided.
- **Organisation** — bringing transactions, budgets, and settlements together.
- **Visibility** — helping users understand spending through summaries and analytics.
- **Accessibility** — providing access through a hosted web application.
